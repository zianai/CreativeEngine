# Custom square delivery requirement

Version: 1.0.0  
Source date: 2026-10-29  
Scope: user_defined

This sample is a Creative Engine operator-defined requirement, not a policy issued by Amazon, eBay, Shopify or another marketplace. Deliver a square PNG at least 512 × 512 pixels and no larger than 20 MiB. The sample demonstrates versioned, sourced delivery rules without claiming marketplace compliance.

To change a requirement, create an updated version of its YAML configuration and retain the provenance date and source. Plans save a complete snapshot and digest; existing plans are not silently updated when this file changes. An official platform rule must cite its authoritative HTTPS source, and the operator remains responsible for checking that source and applicability.
